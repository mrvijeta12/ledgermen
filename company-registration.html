M&A, Market Entry, Incorporation & Dematerialization

M&A Strategy, Due Diligence, and Integration

We support M&A deals end-to-end, from strategy to integration, identifying value creation, synergies, and risks. Our diligence is decision-focused, with integration planning, governance, and KPI mapping, ensuring accelerated realization while maintaining business stability and maximizing deal outcomes.

Joint Ventures and Cross-Border Market Entry

We help structure joint ventures and cross-border market entry with clear governance, IP, tax, regulatory, and cultural alignment. Phased localization, performance triggers, and integration across finance, tax, and compliance ensure smooth operations, risk mitigation, and long-term success.

Company Registration in India and US–India Setup

We provide complete company registration services in India and the US, advising on entity selection, tax alignment, banking/KYC, compliance, IP protection, and investor readiness. End-to-end workflows ensure seamless incorporation, operational setup, and smooth intercompany and regulatory compliance.

Dematerialization of Securities (Rule 9B)

We manage Rule 9B dematerialization for private companies, transitioning physical certificates to digital, cleaning cap tables, resolving anomalies, implementing controls, and coordinating corporate actions. Shareholder communication, compliance, and operational efficiency are ensured throughout the process.
