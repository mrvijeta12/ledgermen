Can Russian Nationals Invest in the Indian Equity Market in 2025? A Complete Updated Guide

Global capital flows always chase two things: growth and stability. And in 2025, India is delivering both at a scale unmatched by most major economies.

Naturally, this has triggered rising interest from investors across the world — including Russia, where individuals and institutions are actively seeking new, high-growth markets.

This leads to one common question:
“Can Russian nationals legally invest in India’s equity markets today?”

Until recently, the answer felt uncertain. Sanctions, compliance hurdles, and global restrictions often created confusion.

But today, the picture is clear — and significantly more exciting.

The Short Answer: Yes, Russian Nationals Can Invest in Indian Equities

India does not restrict Russian citizens from investing in its stock market.

In fact, 2025 has seen new developments that make entry even easier:

• Russian investors have successfully obtained SEBI-approved FPI (Foreign Portfolio Investor) licences.

• Sberbank launched the First India Fund, giving retail Russian investors exposure to the Nifty 50.

• SEBI is simplifying FPI compliance norms to attract more foreign participation.

These developments confirm that India remains open, accessible, and welcoming to Russian capital — provided regulatory norms are met.

What Changed Recently? (2023–2025 Updates)
1. Russian Entities Receive FPI Licences

Multiple Russia-based entities — including investment firms and individuals — have been approved by SEBI under the FPI framework.

This means they can:

• Buy Indian stocks directly.

• Participate in listed debt.

• Trade in permitted derivatives.

• Invest in ETFs.

2. Sberbank Opens Nifty-Linked Fund for Russian Retail Investors

In 2025, Sberbank launched a dedicated mutual fund offering direct exposure to Indian markets, primarily via the Nifty 50 index.

This is a huge milestone, as it:

• Removes the need for individual FPI registration.

• Gives smaller Russian investors a safe, regulated route into Indian equities.

• Validates India as a trusted long-term investment destination.

3. SEBI Simplifies FPI Onboarding

SEBI’s new framework aims to:

• Streamline documentation.

• Standardize KYC norms.

• Ease compliance.

• Bring faster approval timelines.

For Russian investors, this means smoother and more predictable access.

How Russian Nationals Can Invest: Step-By-Step Process

There are two main routes:

Route 1: Direct Investment via FPI Registration

This is suitable for:

• High-net-worth individuals

• Investment firms

• Family offices

Institutional investors

Steps Involved:

1. Choose a SEBI-authorized custodian

2. Submit documents:

Passport:

• Overseas address

• Tax residency details

• Bank reference

3. Complete FATF-aligned KYC

4. Open:

• FPI Bank Account

• Demat Account

• Trading Account

5. Begin investing under the FPI rulebook

• Compliance

• AML verification

• Beneficial ownership disclosure

• Source-of-funds confirmation

Route 2: Investment via Sberbank’s First India Fund (Easiest Route)

Perfect for:

• Retail investors

• Smaller wealth holders

• First-time international investors

Why it matters:

• No need to register as an FPI

• Lower documentation

• Managed professionally

• Exposure to the Nifty 50 and Indian blue-chips

This is currently the simplest and fastest way for Russian citizens to access Indian markets.

Taxation for Russian FPIs

1. Capital Gains

• Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): Taxed as per FPI norms

• Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): Lower tax after 1 year

2. Dividends:

Taxable under Indian regime

3. Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

Applicable on all trades on Indian stock exchanges.
