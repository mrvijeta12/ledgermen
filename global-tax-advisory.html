United Arab Emirates – Corporate Tax (9%) & Free Zones

Effective from 2023, most UAE businesses are subject to a flat 9% corporate tax on profits beyond threshold limits. We help you:

  • Align VAT and customs treatment with corporate tax rules.
  • Manage board/governance reporting for group structures.

Global Knowledge Hub

Direct vs Indirect Tax

Income Tax/Corporate Tax (direct) vs GST/VAT/Customs (indirect).

Examples

Direct tax → salary, business profits, capital gains. Indirect tax → GST on goods, VAT on services.

Double Taxation

Direct double taxation is mitigated by tax treaties. Indirect double taxation is minimized via ITC mechanisms.
