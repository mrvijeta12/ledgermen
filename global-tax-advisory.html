Direct vs Indirect Tax

Income Tax/Corporate Tax (direct) vs GST/VAT/Customs (indirect).

Examples

Direct tax → salary, business profits, capital gains. Indirect tax → GST on goods, VAT on services.

Double Taxation

Direct double taxation is mitigated by tax treaties. Indirect double taxation is minimized via ITC mechanisms.
